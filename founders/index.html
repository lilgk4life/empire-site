I'm Malik, founder of Around The Block Studios. I sank thousands of dollars into games like this one, sometimes as support, sometimes as a foundation for my faction, only to reach a major event and realize there's always a bigger spender who could crush me at a moment's notice and set me back weeks or months. So I set out to make something different: something special, something we could all enjoy for the right reasons, without feeling like we had to be rich to compete.

Our first game, Skyline Kings: Crime Empire, matches you against players who spend like you. Paying never buys first place.

Build a hidden criminal empire in one city at night. Every business is a front, and seven rival families hold the districts you need.

We're opening a 14-day closed test with 25 seats.

What you get: Play on at least 10 of the 14 days and you're a Founder for good: the platinum mark by your name, the plate on your HQ door, and your save carried into Early Access. And when real families arrive, any family you lead gets a gilded banner and seal, is shown first to new players looking for a family, and gives every newcomer who joins a welcome bonus for their first two weeks. Nobody after the test can ever earn it.

What we ask: Here's my real hope: break it. Play every part of it, push every system as hard as it'll go, and find what we missed. That's the true test of what we've built.

  • You're 17 or older.
  • A played day is 10 minutes and three moves.
  • Tell us what you find, good and bad.
  • The build, screenshots, video and story stay private until Early Access. You'll agree to this when you sign up.

How it works: Sign up below. We pick testers and alternates before the test opens, and we email everyone who signed up, picked or not.

This is only the beginning.

What you'll play first is one city, seven families, and your empire from the streets to the skyline. Here's where it goes from there:

  • Next: raid other players' real empires, always matched with players who spend like you, and new Legends to earn.
  • After that: real families. Join up with other players, talk, plan, and fight for the city together.
  • And more cities as the game grows.

I hope you'll stick around, tell us what you'd build next, and help shape what this becomes.

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026 · Last change: November 3, 2026 (see the change log at the bottom)

Who we are

Our game is made by Around The Block Studios LLC, Michigan, United States. Questions and requests: info@aroundtheblockstudios.com.

The short version

Our game needs very little about you to work: an account id the game makes for you, your saved game, and your scores. Play data that helps us improve the game is optional and has a switch in Settings. The game never asks for your real name, email, contacts or location (the family name you choose for the Standings can be anything), we show no ads, and we sell nothing about you to anyone. If you sign up to test the game, the sign-up form asks for a little more; see Founders sign-up below. The test's surveys are optional and anonymous; see Surveys and feedback.

What the game collects, and why

  1. An account id. When you first open the game, the game creates an anonymous account with a random id so your empire can be saved and found again. It is not your name or email. In the game it's called your ledger number (Settings → About). Needed for the game to work.
  2. Your saved game. Your buildings, levels, cash, gems, Operators, districts, timers and settings that belong to the account (not the ones that belong to the phone), saved to your account so it follows you to another phone. Needed for the game to work.
  3. Your scores and Play records for the weekly events. The points you score, the record of each scored job (which target, your plan, the result) so the game can check and rank it, and your display name (the name on the door: the family name you chose) as other players see it on the Standings. Needed for the Standings to work; you can play without ever scoring.
  4. Play data, optional. The first time you open the game you choose whether to share it; nothing is chosen for you, and nothing leaves your phone until you choose to share. If you share (the switch "Help improve the game" in Settings), the game sends events about how you play: what you built and when, how long you played, which screens you opened, errors and clock changes, along with your phone model and OS version. Never your name, contacts, messages, photos or location. You can turn it off any time; it stops immediately. During the closed test, if you share play data, the game adds a random study number to those events so we can see how the test went for each tester. It isn't your name, your email or a device ID. It's kept with your game account's play data at Unity, our analytics provider, and our own reports use only the number. "Clear my play data only" clears both the number and that play data. With play data on, the game also notes how long it and its downloads take to load, which connection the downloads used, and a rough size of your phone's memory.
  5. Crash reports: this test build doesn't send automatic crash reports. A few in-game error notes are part of play data, so they're sent only if you share play data. You can always send us a bug report yourself at info@aroundtheblockstudios.com.

During the closed test, the game counts the days you play (time in the game and moves made) to decide the Founder reward. This runs whether or not you share play data. It's kept with your game account on our game server, not in play data, so "Clear my play data only" doesn't reset it.

Your game account has a random ID, not your name or email. We could match it to you if you tell us your family name or your Key username, for example in Discord or a bug report.

What the game never collects

Your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, calendar, photos, files, messages, microphone, camera, precise or coarse location, advertising identifiers, or anything from other apps. The game shows no ads and has no ad SDK.

Who handles the data

Our game runs on Unity Gaming Services, made by Unity Technologies, which stores the data above for us: Unity Authentication (your account id), Unity Cloud Save (your saved game), Unity Cloud Code and Unity Leaderboards (scores, Play records, rankings), and Unity Analytics (the optional play data). Unity processes it on our behalf under its own terms; we don't give it to anyone else. Google Play handles the download and, when the game sells anything, the purchase; we receive purchase confirmations, never your card. Apple handles the download and, when the game sells anything, the purchase; Apple's Game Center keeps the link if you sign the Books with it; we receive purchase confirmations, never your card.

We use AI assistants (from Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI) to help read, count and summarize test feedback. They may see survey answers and copies of tester-channel posts, including Discord usernames. The summaries they help write keep no names. These companies handle what we share under their own terms.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no trackers and no analytics. Our host (GitHub Pages) keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; we don't see or use them. If you email us, we keep the email to answer you.

Founders sign-up (the closed test)

If you sign up at aroundtheblockstudios.com/founders to test our game, the form asks for: your name or a handle; your email address (Google confirms it when you sign in to the form); the email of the Google account on the phone you'll play on; which phone you'll play on, and its model and OS version; that you're 17 or older; how you heard about us; whether you can play on at least 10 of the 14 days and how long you usually play mobile games a day; which strategy games you've played most (optional); your agreement to the tester agreement; and whether you'd like studio news after the test (optional).

The form is a Google Form. Google runs the form under its own terms, and your answers go to a private spreadsheet only the studio can open. We use them only to choose testers and alternates, send invitations, and run the test. We don't sell them or share them, except adding your account email to the store's test list so you can install. If you're picked to play on an iPhone, we'll email you to ask for the email of your Apple account, and use it the same way.

If you aren't picked, we delete your sign-up within 30 days after the test ends. If you play in the test, we delete your sign-up within 30 days after the test ends too; your saved game follows the rules above, through your account id, not your email. If you ticked "Email me studio news," we keep only your email address to send you news, and you can stop it any time by replying or emailing us. To delete your sign-up sooner, email info@aroundtheblockstudios.com from the address you signed up with.

Surveys and feedback during the test

During the closed test we post three short surveys in our Discord's tester channels: one on install day, one a few days in, and one at the end. They're Google Forms. Every question is optional, and answering has nothing to do with becoming a Founder (that's only the days you play). We don't collect your email address on these forms or ask who you are. They collect only the answers you choose to give and the time you sent them. Your answers go to a private spreadsheet only the studio can open. We use them to understand what's fun, what's confusing and what to fix, and we count them. We don't send them into the game's play data or join them to your account. At the end we ask whether we may quote you. If you say yes, we may use your words without your name. If you say no, we never quote you.

If you post in the tester channels on our Discord, your messages and reactions are held by Discord under its own terms. We read them to find bugs and improve the game. Once a week we count the feedback by its tag and write a short summary for the team. The summary has no names or Discord usernames and is kept in the studio's private files. Posting is optional, and you can delete your own messages any time.

To have your survey answers deleted, email info@aroundtheblockstudios.com. The forms don't record who you are, so tell us roughly when you answered and something you wrote, and we'll find and delete it. To have your Discord messages removed from our channels, delete them yourself, or email us and we'll delete them and the copies we made of them, including in the AI tools we use (those companies may keep their own records for a limited time under their terms).

How long we keep it

  • Your account id and saved game: for as long as your account exists. Closed-test players who played on at least 10 of the 14 test days keep their saved game for good, however long they're away; it is deleted only if they ask.
  • Event Play records: 90 days, then deleted. Rankings of past weeks keep a row with your score and display name; if you delete your account, that name is replaced with "Deleted account".
  • Optional play data: Unity Analytics keeps the raw events for 13 months, then deletes them automatically; the summaries and player profile built from them are kept until you delete your account or ask us to. Deleting your account, or "Clear my play data only", asks Unity to delete all of it sooner.
  • Within 7 days of a verified deletion request, we delete the account and ask Unity to delete the play data; Unity finishes within 30 days.
  • Founders sign-up answers: deleted within 30 days after the closed test ends, except the email of anyone who asked for studio news, kept until they say stop.
  • Test survey answers: deleted within 12 months after the closed test ends. The counts and summaries we made from them, with no names, are kept. Quotes you allowed are kept until you ask us to remove them.
  • Weekly Discord feedback summaries: kept until Early Access opens. They hold no names or usernames. Your messages stay on Discord until you or we delete them.

Your choices

  • Turn play data on or off: Settings → "Help improve the game".
  • Change the name other players see: Settings → The Books → the name on the door (once every 30 days).
  • Open your empire on another phone: Settings → The Books → "Show my key".
  • Delete only the optional play data: Settings → The Books → Delete account → "Clear my play data only".
  • Delete your account and everything with it: below.

Deleting your account

In the game: Settings → The Books → Delete account. Hold the button to confirm. Your empire, your key and your place on this week's Standings are erased straight away; past weeks' rankings keep the score, with your name replaced by "Deleted account". The game also asks Unity to delete the play data tied to your account, and Unity finishes that within 30 days. None of it can be brought back. Settings on your phone stay.

Without the game (for example, after uninstalling): email info@aroundtheblockstudios.com, subject "Delete my account", with your ledger number and the name on your door.

To make sure nobody can delete an empire that isn't theirs, we delete only when you send a deletion code: in the game, Settings → The Books → Delete account → "Get a deletion code". It works once and lasts 7 days. Never send us your key.

No longer have the game? You can still ask, and you don't need to reinstall to do it: write to us with your ledger number and the name on your door. We can only delete an account we can prove is yours, and that proof comes from inside the game, so we'll keep your request on file and delete the account as soon as you send a deletion code.

Within 7 days of receiving your code, we delete your account, ask Unity to delete the play data and reply to confirm. Unity finishes within 30 days of our request. Once it's gone, it can't be brought back.

Children

Our game is for players 17 and older. We don't knowingly create accounts for anyone under 17; if we learn that we have, we delete the account. If you believe a child is playing, write to info@aroundtheblockstudios.com.

The test sign-up asks you to confirm you're 17 or older; if we learn someone under 17 signed up, we delete their sign-up.

Security

Everything the game sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). The data sits in Unity's infrastructure, protected by their security measures; no system is perfect, and we'll tell you if something goes wrong.

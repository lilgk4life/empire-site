Delete your account

The fastest way is in the game:
Settings → The Books → Delete account. Hold the button to confirm.

This erases your empire, your key and your place on this week's Standings straight away, and asks Unity to delete the play data tied to your account, which it finishes within 30 days. Past weeks' rankings keep the score with your name replaced by "Deleted account". None of it can be brought back.

No game? Email us instead:

info@aroundtheblockstudios.com, subject "Delete my account", with your ledger number and the name on your door.

To make sure nobody can delete an empire that isn't theirs, we delete only when you send a deletion code: in the game, Settings → The Books → Delete account → "Get a deletion code". It works once and lasts 7 days. Never send us your key.

No longer have the game? You can still ask, and you don't need to reinstall to do it: write to us with your ledger number and the name on your door. We can only delete an account we can prove is yours, and that proof comes from inside the game, so we'll keep your request on file and delete the account as soon as you send a deletion code.

Within 7 days of receiving your code, we delete your account, ask Unity to delete the play data and reply to confirm. Unity finishes within 30 days of our request. Once it's gone, it can't be brought back.

Only want to clear your optional play data and keep your empire?

In the game: Settings → The Books → Delete account → "Clear my play data only".
